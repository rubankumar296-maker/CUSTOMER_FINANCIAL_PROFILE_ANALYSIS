# Business Questions — Customer Financial Profile Analysis

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer demographic and financial information to identify patterns in income, savings, credit scores, loan amounts, employment status, and supplied risk classifications.

## 2. Customer Demographics

1. How many unique customers are included in the dataset?
2. How are customers distributed by gender?
3. Which age groups contain the most customers?
4. What is the average customer age?

## 3. Income and Employment

1. What are the average, minimum, and maximum annual incomes?
2. Which employment category has the highest average annual income?
3. How does customer income vary across employment categories?
4. How does income vary across age groups?

## 4. Savings and Loans

1. What is the average customer savings balance?
2. What is the average loan amount?
3. How do savings balances vary across age groups?
4. Is there an observable relationship between annual income and savings balance?
5. How do recorded loan amounts compare with savings balances?

## 5. Credit Scores and Risk Categories

1. What is the distribution of credit scores?
2. How many customers are classified as Low, Medium, and High risk?
3. What percentage of customers belongs to each supplied risk category?
4. How do average credit scores differ across risk categories?
5. How do risk classifications vary across employment categories?
6. How do risk classifications vary across age groups?

## 6. Dashboard and Reporting

1. Which KPIs best summarize the customer financial profile?
2. Can users explore results through gender, employment, age-group, and risk filters?
3. Do Power BI results agree with Python and SQL results?

## 7. Analytical Limitations

The analysis is descriptive. The dataset contains supplied risk labels, but this project does not establish actual loan-default outcomes or prove that one customer characteristic causes another.

## 8. Expected Deliverables

- Validated dataset
- Python notebooks and visualizations
- SQL business analysis
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Documented findings and recommendations
